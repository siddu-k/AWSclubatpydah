Who We Are

Building the Future of Cloud at Pydah

The AWS Student Builder Group at Pydah College of Engineering is a premier technical community dedicated to advancing cloud computing knowledge among students. We bridge the gap between academic learning and industry requirements.

Through hands-on workshops, hackathons, and real-world projects, we prepare our members to become certified AWS professionals and innovative cloud architects ready for the modern tech landscape.
