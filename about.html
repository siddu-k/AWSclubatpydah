Who We Are

About the AWS Student Builder Group

We are a passionate community of cloud enthusiasts at Pydah College of Engineering, dedicated to learning, building, and innovating with Amazon Web Services.

Our Mission

To empower students with practical, hands-on cloud computing skills. We bridge the gap between academic theory and industry reality by providing access to AWS resources, expert mentorship, and real-world projects.

Our Vision

To cultivate the next generation of cloud leaders and innovators. We envision a campus where every student has the confidence and technical capability to architect scalable, secure, and modern cloud solutions.

What We Do

Hands-On Workshops

Learn AWS services like EC2, S3, and Lambda through interactive, guided labs.

Hackathons

Compete in high-energy building challenges to solve real problems using cloud tech.

Certifications

Study groups and resources to help you pass AWS Cloud Practitioner and Solutions Architect exams.
